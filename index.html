How it works

From lesson plan to headset

Four steps. Your instructors stay involved the whole way, because they know the job and the students.

  1. 01Scope

    Pick the task

    We sit down with your instructors and choose the skill that would benefit most from more practice.

  2. 02Build

    Make the module

    We script it from your procedures, build it, and show you working drafts along the way.

  3. 03Pilot

    Try it with a class

    Students run it, instructors give feedback, and we fix what doesn't match the real job.

  4. 04Support

    Keep it running

    We help with setup, train your staff, and update the module when procedures change.
